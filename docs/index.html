Command reference

Everything Beacon can do.

Commands are synced automatically whenever the bot starts or joins a new server.

/helpShow Beacon commands and the most important quick actions.
/quickstartShow the recommended setup flow for a new server.
/setupConfigure welcome, logs, onboarding and member roles.
/healthCalculate a community health score with next actions.
/dashboardOpen a live server snapshot with quick controls.
/announcePublish a polished custom announcement embed.
/onboardingCreate a button-based onboarding experience.
/dmwelcomeWelcome new members with a configurable direct message.
/ticketsetupConfigure private support tickets and transcripts.
/ticketpanelPost the public panel used to open tickets.
/ticketcloseClose a ticket and deliver its transcript.
/ticketaddAdd another member to the current ticket.
/ticketremoveRemove a member from the current ticket.
/ticketrenameRename the current ticket channel.
/ticketinfoInspect ticket ownership, claim state and age.
/ticketstatsReview support volume and ticket workload.
/rolepanelCreate a self-service role selection panel.
/eventPublish an event with RSVP controls.
/memberView a member's community profile.
/settingsReview the active Beacon configuration.
/statusCheck uptime, ping and connected servers.

Privacy

Beacon stores only the configuration and operational data required to provide its Discord features. Authentication data is used to identify your Discord account and create a secure website session.

Terms

Use Beacon responsibly and in accordance with Discord's terms. Server administrators remain responsible for their configuration, permissions and community content.
