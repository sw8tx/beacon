1. Scope and transparency

This policy describes how Beacon may handle information when you visit the website, authenticate with Discord, invite or use the bot, access the dashboard, participate in a promotion, or contact support. It is intended to explain the practical flow of information in clear language.

Beacon is independent from Discord Inc. Discord separately controls information processed on its own platform under its own terms and privacy policy. This policy covers Beacon-controlled services and the information Beacon receives through permitted integrations.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on the feature, Beacon may receive Discord user and server identifiers, usernames, display names, avatar information, roles, permissions, configuration choices, command activity, interaction history, and operational logs. We may also receive information you choose to send to support, such as an email address, a description of an issue, screenshots, or diagnostic details.

Our systems may automatically record technical information such as approximate timestamps, request paths, browser type, device information, error details, and security events. We try to limit collection to what is reasonably needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

3. Why we use information

We use information to authenticate requests, provide bot and dashboard functionality, apply server settings, display statistics, deliver rewards, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, protect accounts, measure reliability, and communicate about support requests or material service changes.

We may combine operational records for security and reliability analysis, but we do not sell personal information. We do not use Discord information to make decisions unrelated to the Beacon feature you choose to use.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, storage, authentication, monitoring, security, analytics, and support providers that help us run Beacon. These providers are expected to process information only for the services they provide and to apply appropriate safeguards.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, to investigate abuse, to enforce our terms, or in connection with a business reorganization. We do not share private information for unrelated advertising or sell it to data brokers.

5. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose collected, ongoing service operation, security investigations, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or legitimate records. Retention can vary by feature and by whether data is part of a live configuration, an audit record, or a support conversation.

We use access controls, authentication, logging, and other safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending passwords, tokens, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary support channels.

6. Your choices and requests

You may remove Beacon from a server, sign out of the dashboard, change server settings, clear local browser storage, or contact support about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions. We may need reasonable information to verify a request and protect another person's account.

Some information may remain where it is needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or an unresolved dispute. Where a request conflicts with those needs, we will explain the applicable limitation as far as permitted.

7. Children and policy changes

Beacon is not designed to knowingly collect information directly from children in violation of applicable law. Server owners and administrators are responsible for using Discord and Beacon in a way that follows the rules that apply to their community.

We may update this policy when our features, providers, or legal obligations change. The updated version will show a new date. If a change is material, we may provide additional notice through the website or another appropriate channel.
