3. Server administrators and Art. 28 GDPR

For tickets, member records, logs, moderation records and similar data that an administrator causes Beacon to process for a Discord server, the server operator may be the controller and Beacon may act as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The exact role depends on the purpose, instructions and actual configuration.

Beacon will process such data only to provide the configured feature and under the administrator's documented instructions. Where Art. 28 GDPR applies, use of the feature requires an appropriate data-processing agreement (AVV/DPA) with Beacon before processing starts. This Privacy Policy is not an AVV/DPA. Server administrators must also provide their own notices and lawful instructions to members.

4. Providers and international transfers

Beacon uses Discord for authentication and platform delivery, Cloudflare for hosting, edge delivery and/or storage where configured, and email, security or monitoring providers only where needed for the service. Providers receive the data necessary for their role and must be bound by appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

Some providers may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where required, Beacon relies on an adequacy decision or suitable safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. You may request information about the applicable safeguard by contacting us.

5. Cookies, local storage and rights

The website uses only technically necessary browser storage for the selected language and requested login/session functions. No non-essential analytics or advertising tracker is loaded by Beacon. The legal basis for associated personal-data processing is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for security; storage access is governed additionally by § 25 TDDDG.

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may complain to a data-protection supervisory authority. Requests go to help@beacon-bot.site; we may verify identity before acting.
