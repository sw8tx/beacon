Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains what information Beacon may receive when you use the website, dashboard, Discord bot, and connected features, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we use

Depending on the feature, Beacon may process Discord user and server identifiers, usernames, avatar information, configuration choices, command activity, and operational logs. We use this information to authenticate requests, provide bot features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers needed to host, secure, and operate Beacon. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, security, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.

Your choices

You may stop using Beacon, remove the bot from a server, sign out of the dashboard, or contact support about access, correction, or deletion requests. Some records may need to be retained where required for security or law.

Discord and third parties

Beacon is integrated with Discord, but is independent from Discord Inc. Discord's own terms and privacy policy apply to information processed by Discord.
