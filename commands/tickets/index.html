Ticket Commands

Set up a clean support flow for your server. Configure the panel once, let members open private tickets, and keep every support case in one place.

How it works

When someone opens a ticket, Beacon asks for a short subject and details, then creates a private channel for the member and your staff team. Staff can claim the ticket, add another person if needed, and close it when the issue is done.

1

Configure

Use /ticketsetup to set support role, panel text, labels, limits, transcript behavior, and the accent color.

2

Post panel

Use /ticketpanel in the channel where members should open tickets. Beacon posts the embed and button.

3

Handle ticket

Staff can claim, add users, check info, and close the channel once the support case is finished.

/ticketsetup options

Click an option to see what it changes.

support_role

Staff role that can see and handle opened tickets.

panel_title

Main title on the public ticket panel.

panel_message

Short text telling members what the ticket panel is for.

panel_rules

Small note before opening, like no duplicate tickets.

button_label

Text on the open button. Default is Open Ticket.

claim_label

Text on the staff claim button inside a ticket.

close_label

Text on the close button inside a ticket.

welcome_title

Title shown when a ticket channel is created.

welcome_message

First message inside the ticket. Supports {user} and {server}.

name_format

Channel name format, for example ticket-{username}.

max_open

How many open tickets one member can have at the same time.

dm_transcript

Sends the transcript by DM after closing, when possible.
