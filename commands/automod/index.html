Automod

Choose the channels and rules Beacon should watch. A removed message triggers a short ten-second timeout and a private warning DM; while that timeout is active, repeated violations are removed without endlessly extending it.

/automod-configure

Set channel, spam, links, images, spam pings, action, timeout duration, and whether to enable now.

/automod-enable / disable

Turn the saved policy on or off without losing any of its settings.

/automod-status

Show the current scope and action privately to staff. Use /log-setup for central audit records and TXT files.
