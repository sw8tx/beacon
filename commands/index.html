Ticket Commands

Set up a clean support flow for your server. Configure the panel once, let members open private tickets, and keep every support case in one place.

How it works

When someone opens a ticket, Beacon asks for a short subject and details, then creates a private channel for the member and your staff team. Simple, organized, and easy to close with a transcript when the issue is done.
