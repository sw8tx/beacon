Copyright Dispute

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Respecting creative work

Beacon respects copyright and expects users to do the same. Website text, code, designs, logos, graphics, and other material may be protected by copyright or other rights.

How to submit a notice

If you believe content available through Beacon infringes your copyright, send a clear notice to the Beacon support team. Include your name and contact details, identification of the work, the exact location of the material, a statement of good-faith belief, and a statement that the information is accurate.

Review process

We may request more information, restrict access while reviewing a report, or contact the relevant account owner. False or incomplete reports may delay review. This page does not replace advice from a qualified legal professional.
