GDPR Notice

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Our approach

For people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Beacon aims to process personal data fairly, transparently, and only for defined operational purposes.

Legal bases

Depending on the context, processing may be based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as security and product reliability, consent where required, or a legal obligation.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and complain to a supervisory authority.

Making a request

Contact Beacon support with enough information for us to understand and verify your request. We may ask for reasonable confirmation of identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law.
