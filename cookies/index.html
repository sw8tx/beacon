Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

What cookies do

Cookies and similar browser storage help a website remember preferences, keep sessions working, understand basic performance, and protect features from abuse.

Beacon storage

Beacon may use essential storage for authentication, language preferences, security, and small interface states such as a claimed promotional badge. We aim to avoid storing more than the feature needs.

Your controls

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent login, preferences, or other features from working correctly. Where consent is required, non-essential technologies should only be used after the appropriate choice.
